Risk-free rate source: BSP benchmark rate for 10-year Treasury bonds. Check tradingeconomics.com/philippines/government-bond-yield for current yield.
ERP source: Use Damodaran's implied ERP for the Philippines, published each January at pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/ → "Country Risk Premium" spreadsheet. This is a forward-looking estimate derived from current PSEi prices and consensus earnings — not a historical average.
Why it matters: A higher ERP raises the discount rate, which lowers every intrinsic value estimate and tightens the MoS threshold. Damodaran's January 2026 implied ERP for the Philippines is approximately 7.6% (standalone) or 5.5–6% as a mature-market add-on.
